Maison Tomi

Freshly Baked, Beautifully Styled

At Tomi's Special Bites, we believe every celebration deserves a touch of luxury. Founded on a passion for outstanding regional flavors, we combine the crunchiest traditional Nigerian Chin-Chin recipe with modern cake artistry.

We source local organic ingredients, use rich cream frostings, and assemble bespoke gift hampers designed to wow your guests. Whether it is a wedding, birthday, or a simple craving, we bake to make it unforgettable.
